# Economics

## What a converted lead is worth to Credex

Credex sells discounted AI credits — Cursor, Claude, ChatGPT Enterprise — at roughly
20–40% below retail. A typical deal looks like:

- Company spending $2,000/month on AI tools retail
- Credex can supply the same for $1,400/month (30% discount)
- Company saves $600/month, signs a 12-month commitment
- Credex GMV: $1,400 × 12 = $16,800 per customer per year
- Credex margin: ~15–20% on credits = **$2,520–$3,360 gross profit per customer**

Conservative estimate: **$2,500 gross profit per converted customer.**

For high-savings audits (>$500/mo identified savings), the conversion likelihood is
higher because the pain is proven and quantified. These leads are worth more —
estimate **$4,000–$5,000 per converted high-savings lead.**

## CAC at each GTM channel

| Channel | Effort | Est. Leads (month 1) | CAC |
|---------|--------|----------------------|-----|
| Hacker News Show HN | 2 hours | 40–80 audit completions, 8–15 emails | $0 |
| Reddit posts (3 subs) | 3 hours | 20–40 completions, 4–8 emails | $0 |
| X cold DMs | 5 hours/week | 10–20 completions, 2–4 emails | $0 |
| Credex existing customer email | 1 hour | 30–60 completions, 10–20 emails | $0 |
| Shareable result URLs (viral) | 0 hours | Compounds over time | $0 |

All channels are $0 paid CAC. The only cost is time. At scale, if Credex hires a
growth person at $60k/year and they drive 500 leads/month, CAC = $10/lead.
