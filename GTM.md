# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Exact Target User

Not "startups" — specifically: **Engineering Managers at Series A startups (10–50 employees)** who manage a team of 3–8 developers and have budget approval for tooling. They are paying $500–$5000/month across AI tools, they got the tools approved one by one over 18 months, and they have never done a consolidated audit. They feel vaguely guilty about the spend but do not have time to research alternatives.

Secondary user: **Indie hackers and solo founders** running a side project with 1–3 AI subscriptions who are cost-sensitive and will share interesting tools with their network.

## What they Google right before wanting this tool

- "cursor vs github copilot 2026"
- "chatgpt team plan worth it"
- "claude pro vs team plan difference"
- "how much does ai tooling cost per developer"
- "reduce saas spend startup"
- "ai tools for developers cost comparison"

These are high-intent searches from people already questioning their spend.

## Where they hang out online

- **r/ExperiencedDevs** and **r/cscareerquestions** — engineers discussing tooling costs
- **r/SideProject** and **r/indiehackers** — solo founders tracking expenses
- **Hacker News** — Show HN posts get traction if the tool is genuinely useful
- **Slack: Indie Hackers community** — active discussion on tools and costs
- **X/Twitter lists** — "AI tools" and "developer tools" lists followed by engineering managers
- **Linear, Notion, and Vercel Discord servers** — developers who care about their stack

## How to get the first 100 users in 30 days with $0

**Week 1 — Launch on Hacker News:**
Post "Show HN: Free tool to audit your AI tool spend" on a Tuesday morning at 9am EST. The title must lead with the value, not the tech. Target 50 upvotes to hit the front page. Respond to every comment personally.

**Week 2 — Reddit:**
Post in r/SideProject ("Built a free AI spend auditor — here is what I found about my own stack"), r/ExperiencedDevs, and r/artificial. Do not spam — one authentic post per subreddit with real findings from running your own audit.

**Week 3 — Cold DM on X:**
Search X for tweets complaining about AI tool costs. Reply with the tool link in context. Target 20 genuine replies per day. Do not blast — be specific to their situation.

**Week 4 — Referral from shareable URLs:**
By week 4, users who completed audits have shareable result URLs. Encourage sharing by adding "Share your audit result" button on the result page. Each shared link is a free acquisition.

## The unfair distribution channel

Credex already has relationships with companies that overpurchased AI credits. Those companies have finance teams actively looking to recoup value. Credex can email its existing customer list: "We built a free tool to audit your team's AI spend — here is your private link." This is warm distribution that no competitor can replicate.

- 500+ audit completions
- 15%+ email capture rate (75+ leads)
- 3+ organic shares on X or HN from users sharing their result URL
- At least 2 inbound messages asking about Credex credits
- Zero paid spend
