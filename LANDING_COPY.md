## FAQ

**Q: Is this actually free?**
A: Yes. No credit card, no login, no trial period. The audit is free because Credex makes money when users with significant savings switch to Credex credits — not from charging you.

**Q: How accurate is the pricing data?**
A: Every price in the audit engine is pulled from the official vendor pricing page and verified weekly. Sources are cited in our public PRICING_DATA.md file on GitHub.

**Q: Do you store my data?**
A: We only store your email if you choose to submit it for the report. We do not store your company name or spend numbers unless you provide them. The audit runs in your browser.

**Q: What AI tools do you cover?**
A: Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Claude, ChatGPT, Anthropic API, OpenAI API, Gemini, and Windsurf. We add new tools as they become relevant.

**Q: Why would Credex build a free tool that shows me cheaper alternatives?**
A: Because honest advice builds trust. If you are already on the optimal plan, we will tell you. If you are overspending significantly, Credex credits are often the best solution — but we only surface that when it genuinely applies.
